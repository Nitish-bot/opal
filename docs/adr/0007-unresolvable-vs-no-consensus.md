# Distinguish an unresolvable assertion from a vote with no consensus

`Unresolvable` is an affirmative resolution that an assertion cannot be decided under its Resolution Spec; it assigns fault to the asserter and participates in ordinary slashing and rewards. `NoConsensus` is a vote-only outcome produced when the dual Vote Quorum is not met or, after quorum, none of `True`, `False`, or `Unresolvable` reaches the 67% supermajority. The Devnet quorum requires at least 3,000 USDC in total voting stake and at least 10 distinct voting wallet addresses; a zero-Vote round necessarily fails it. `NoConsensus` settles no-fault, making every Participant Bond and voting stake claimable minus ordinary fees, with no rewards. It reuses the retired `TooEarly` outcome code (`2`) before Devnet deployment, while `Unresolvable` remains code `3`. This supersedes [ADR-0005](0005-no-fault-unresolvable.md).

**Why.** Treating every indeterminate result as no-fault gave a voter no economic reason to choose `Unresolvable`: that position could lose but could never win. Treating `Unresolvable` as the automatic winner whenever no option reached 67% created the opposite exploit—a tiny `Unresolvable` position could capture nearly the entire opposing stake. The distinction lets a quorum-backed 67% `Unresolvable` supermajority win honestly while recognizing that a failed quorum or a vote with no supermajority identified no correct voter side. A zero-Vote round likewise identified no winning side and follows the same no-fault result rather than affirmatively judging the assertion `Unresolvable`.

**Consequences.** An asserter is incorrect whenever the final outcome is not `True`, except that `NoConsensus` assigns no fault to anyone. The canonical prediction-market demo handles both as invalid market results while still exposing their different meanings and participant settlements; see [ADR-0019](0019-prediction-market-invalid-result-mapping.md). Ordinary 1% Bond Fees and 0.1% Voting Fees apply to `NoConsensus` refunds.
