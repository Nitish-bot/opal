# Terminate stalled resolver requests fee-free after 24 hours

If the trusted LLM resolver does not post a verdict within 24 hours after the first dispute is accepted, the Assertion becomes eligible for the exceptional terminal status `ResolverUnavailable`. Anyone may invoke the deterministic timeout finalizer at or after the deadline; the caller cannot select an outcome or redirect funds and normally pays the Solana execution cost. The transaction atomically refunds the full 500 USDC Participant Bond to both the asserter and LLM disputer. No Bond Fee, slashing, or reward applies. Resolver verdicts are accepted only before the deadline and are rejected afterward.

`ResolverUnavailable` is an operational lifecycle status, not a fifth Resolution Outcome. It keeps `outcome = None` because the protocol made no rubric-relative truth judgment. Normal resolution produces exactly one of `True`, `False`, `Unresolvable`, or `NoConsensus`. Public documentation presents resolver unavailability as an edge case and does not turn the intended `99.9%` normal-completion expectation into a measured availability guarantee without supporting production telemetry.

**Why.** Without a protocol timeout, a resolver outage can leave 1,000 USDC across two valid Participant Bonds locked indefinitely. Reusing `Unresolvable` would falsely assign fault to the asserter, while reusing vote-only `NoConsensus` would erase the distinction between a failed vote and an unavailable service. Charging the ordinary Bond Fee would make both participants pay Opal for a resolution service it failed to complete. A bounded, permissionless, fee-free terminal path releases funds without letting the timeout caller influence truth or settlement.

**Consequences.** The Devnet target adds a checked resolver deadline, a terminal resolver-unavailable state, and a permissionless timeout-finalization instruction with atomic public-wallet refunds. This path is the sole exception to the rule that every accepted Participant Bond pays the 1% Bond Fee. External transaction and account-creation costs remain outside the bond refund and are not reimbursed by Opal. Integrators must distinguish a normal `Resolved` Assertion from this operational failure and apply their own invalid-or-retry behavior; the canonical prediction-market demo voids the market while preserving `ResolverUnavailable` as the exact reason ([ADR-0019](0019-prediction-market-invalid-result-mapping.md)). The original Assertion remains a permanent public record and cannot later receive a truth outcome.
