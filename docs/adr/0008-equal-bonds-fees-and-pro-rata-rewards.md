# Equal participant bonds, explicit fees, and capital-weighted rewards

The Devnet MVP uses a fixed 500 USDC Participant Bond for the asserter and either disputer, with a 1% Bond Fee withheld from every bond at normal settlement and a 0.1% Voting Fee withheld from voting stake. The exceptional `ResolverUnavailable` operational path later established by [ADR-0012](0012-resolver-unavailable.md) refunds the asserter and LLM disputer bonds in full because Opal did not complete the resolution service. The three bonded roles in one Assertion must be held by three distinct wallet addresses, and each of those wallets remains eligible to cast its one Vote for any outcome. Before voting, the single correct side receives its own 495 USDC refund plus the incorrect side's remaining 495 USDC bond. A vote can assign fault only after its dual Vote Quorum is met: at least 3,000 USDC in total voting stake and at least 10 distinct voting wallets. When an assertion escalates to voting, all three bonds and all voting stake are co-located in one PER-resident Vote Settlement Vault. Incorrect bonds and losing voting stake form a Slashed Pool after fees; that pool is distributed pro rata among correct bonded participants and winning voters according to original capital at risk. A bonded wallet's bond and voting stake are separate positions: each earns Reward Weight when correct and is slashed when incorrect. Vote finalization makes each payout claimable; the recipient must claim it into their reusable Private Voting Balance, and withdrawal to a public Solana wallet remains a separate manual action. Opal takes no additional treasury share. Each wallet may cast one immutable vote per Vote Round, with a 1 USDC minimum and no maximum.

**Why.** Equal bonds give each assertion and dispute the same economic commitment and remove arbitrary escalation ratios. Requiring distinct bonded wallets prevents one wallet from self-challenging, manufacturing escalation, or occupying both sides of the same bonded contest. It is not a proof-of-personhood mechanism. Once voting begins, prohibiting a bonded wallet from voting or restricting its outcome would add role-specific rules that are trivially bypassed with another wallet, so its bond and Vote instead settle independently. Explicit fees pay the protocol for every resolution attempt, including a no-fault `NoConsensus`, without obscuring how much collateral is at risk. A bilateral dispute needs no role-based allocation: one correct counterparty can receive the remaining losing bond directly. Once voters participate, original capital at risk supplies one common reward unit across roles and eliminates fixed reward percentages that could overpay a role regardless of its actual risk. A wallet-level maximum would be bypassed by splitting stake across wallets, so security remains linear stake plus slashing.

**Consequences.** A 500 USDC bond refunds at most 495 USDC, and voting stake refunds at most 99.9%. Voting Fee deduction occurs after tallying; quorum, the 67% result, and voter Reward Weight use original locked stake. A Voting Fee that is not exactly representable in USDC atomic units rounds down, leaving the sub-micro-USDC remainder with the participant. The current `ProtocolConfig` bond-ratio, single-fee, and role-specific reward-share fields are legacy and must be replaced before Devnet deployment. Vote finalization must deduct fees before constructing the Slashed Pool, freeze every claimable amount, and use checked pro-rata arithmetic. Vote Initialization must co-locate the public Bond Vault and private vote funds before starting the Voting Window; Opal sponsors missing private payout balances for bonded participants, who later claim into those balances rather than receiving automatic public-wallet payouts. These setup costs are an operational expense and do not alter the 1% Bond Fee. Opal charges no protocol fee on Private Voting Balance deposits, payout claims, or withdrawals; external execution costs remain separate. `NoConsensus` remains the sole no-fault exception and pays no rewards ([ADR-0007](0007-unresolvable-vs-no-consensus.md)). Vote-stage claim delivery is specified separately in [ADR-0009](0009-claim-based-vote-payouts.md).
