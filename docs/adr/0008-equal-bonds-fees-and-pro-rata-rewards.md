# Equal participant bonds, explicit fees, and capital-weighted rewards

The Devnet MVP uses a fixed 500 USDC Participant Bond for the asserter and either disputer, with a 1% Bond Fee withheld from every bond at normal settlement and a 0.1% Voting Fee withheld from voting stake. The exceptional `ResolverUnavailable` operational path later established by [ADR-0012](0012-resolver-unavailable.md) refunds the asserter and LLM disputer bonds in full because Opal did not complete the resolution service. The three bonded roles in one Assertion must be held by three distinct wallet addresses, and each of those wallets remains eligible to cast its one Vote for any outcome. Before voting, the single correct side receives its own 495 USDC refund plus the incorrect side's remaining 495 USDC bond. A vote can assign fault only after its dual Vote Quorum is met: at least 3,000 USDC in total voting stake and at least 10 distinct voting wallets. When an assertion escalates to voting, all three bonds are represented before the Voting Window in one round-scoped logical Vote Settlement Vault on the PER; each accepted Vote later moves its stake into that same logical balance. Actual USDC custody remains in eSPL's shared per-mint global vault on Solana. Incorrect bonds and losing voting stake form a Slashed Pool after fees; that pool is distributed pro rata among correct bonded participants and winning voters according to original capital at risk. A bonded wallet's bond and voting stake are separate positions: each earns Reward Weight when correct and is slashed when incorrect. Authoritative PER finalization makes each payout claimable; the recipient must claim it into their reusable Private Voting Balance, and withdrawal to a public Solana wallet remains a separate manual action. Opal takes no discretionary treasury share from the Slashed Pool; only the indivisible atomic-unit remainder from pro-rata floors goes to the treasury. Each wallet may cast one immutable vote per Vote Round, with a 1 USDC minimum and no maximum.

**Why.** Equal bonds give each assertion and dispute the same economic commitment and remove arbitrary escalation ratios. Requiring distinct bonded wallets prevents one wallet from self-challenging, manufacturing escalation, or occupying both sides of the same bonded contest. It is not a proof-of-personhood mechanism. Once voting begins, prohibiting a bonded wallet from voting or restricting its outcome would add role-specific rules that are trivially bypassed with another wallet, so its bond and Vote instead settle independently. Explicit fees pay the protocol for every resolution attempt, including a no-fault `NoConsensus`, without obscuring how much collateral is at risk. A bilateral dispute needs no role-based allocation: one correct counterparty can receive the remaining losing bond directly. Once voters participate, original capital at risk supplies one common reward unit across roles and eliminates fixed reward percentages that could overpay a role regardless of its actual risk. A wallet-level maximum would be bypassed by splitting stake across wallets, so security remains linear stake plus slashing.

**Consequences.** A 500 USDC bond refunds at most 495 USDC, and voting stake refunds at most 99.9%. Voting Fee deduction occurs after tallying; quorum, the 67% result, and voter Reward Weight use original locked stake. A Voting Fee that is not exactly representable in USDC atomic units rounds down, leaving the sub-micro-USDC fee remainder with the participant. Reward Weight is also denominated in atomic units: one 500 USDC bond contributes `500_000_000`, and a winning Vote contributes its `gross_stake_atomic`. Each pro-rata reward is `floor(slashed_pool_atomic × reward_weight_atomic ÷ total_reward_weight_atomic)`, using checked `u128` intermediates and a range-checked `u64` result. Finalization freezes per-outcome stake and position counts, all three Bond Fees, the exact accumulated sum of independently floored Voting Fees, the Slashed Pool, Reward Weight, and the number of positive unclaimed positions; it cannot know the sum of every independently floored payout from aggregate weight alone. Each claim decrements that count and records its exact payout. Once the count reaches zero, checked balance and claimed-total reconciliation classifies the remaining non-negative amount—bounded below the rewarded-position count—as treasury-owned pro-rata dust. It is never assigned to a participant. The current `ProtocolConfig` bond-ratio, single-fee, and role-specific reward-share fields are legacy and must be replaced before Devnet deployment. Vote Initialization must represent the complete public Bond Vault balance in the logical settlement balance before starting the Voting Window; voting stakes arrive only as Votes are accepted. The Vote Disputer funds required per-round setup; each voter creates and funds its own canonical Private Voting Balance, while a bonded non-voter may later create and delegate an empty claim destination without depositing USDC. Opal guarantees no sponsor and charges no protocol fee on Private Voting Balance deposits, payout claims, or withdrawals; external execution costs remain separate. `NoConsensus` remains the sole no-fault exception and pays no rewards ([ADR-0007](0007-unresolvable-vs-no-consensus.md)). Vote-stage claim delivery is specified separately in [ADR-0009](0009-claim-based-vote-payouts.md).
