# Require participants to claim vote-stage payouts

When a Vote Round finalizes, Opal freezes the outcome, fees, Slashed Pool, reward inputs, and each position's exact payout entitlement. The amount never changes after finalization and earns no interest, yield, bonus, or other time-based adjustment while unclaimed. Finalization does not attempt to transfer every payout in one transaction. As soon as it confirms, a position with a positive payout becomes `Claimable`; there is no cooldown or separate claim-opening transaction. A fully slashed position becomes terminal `Slashed` and requires no claim. Each eligible recipient must submit a wallet-authorized claim that transfers their finalized payout from the Vote Settlement Vault into the wallet's canonical reusable Private Voting Balance. Once the claim confirms, the credited USDC is immediately unlocked and may fund another Vote or be withdrawn; the claim creates no new lock or cooldown. The claim cannot redirect funds to another private balance or a public token account. Withdrawal from the canonical balance to a public Solana wallet remains a separate action. Settlement paths that end before voting keep their fixed, bounded automatic payouts.

**Why.** A Vote Round has no fixed maximum number of voters, while a Solana transaction has finite account, size, instruction, and compute limits. A single automatic transaction therefore cannot pay an unbounded recipient set. Required claims divide settlement into bounded, independently retryable transactions without an Opal-operated payout worker or an operator-liveness dependency. Every voter already has a Private Voting Balance, and Vote Initialization creates one for a bonded participant who lacks it, so the claim destination is known before finalization. Requiring the payout owner to sign also makes the delivery action explicit while preserving Opal's deterministic onchain calculation.

**Consequences.** A resolved Assertion can have unclaimed payouts. `Resolved` means the truth outcome and payout formula are final, not that every participant has received USDC or that bounded publication of every public Vote position is complete. Each voter position eventually has its own permanent public account with its exact payout amount and `Claimable`, `Claimed`, or `Slashed` status; this does not expose the wallet's total Private Voting Balance. See [ADR-0017](0017-per-voter-public-position-accounts.md). A claim must validate the Vote Round, payout owner, positive entitlement, mint, configured Voting PER, and canonically derived destination Private Voting Balance; transfer and claimed-state update occur atomically, and the same position can never be claimed twice. Arbitrary destinations and zero-value claims are invalid. Unclaimed USDC remains reserved in the Vote Settlement Vault with no forfeiture deadline. The original wallet remains the only claim authority: Opal has no admin recovery, reassignment, or destination override if its signing key is lost. Any recovery must come from the wallet's own external recovery mechanism. Bond and Vote payouts remain separate claim records when one wallet owns both positions, but an integrating client may bundle both claim instructions into one transaction and wallet signature. Opal charges no additional claim fee. The claimant pays any network execution cost unless an integrating application chooses to sponsor it; sponsorship is not a protocol guarantee and does not remove the claimant's signature requirement or change the payout amount.
