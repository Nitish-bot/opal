# Require participants to claim vote-stage payouts

When authoritative PER settlement finalizes a Vote Round, Opal freezes the outcome, fees, Slashed Pool, per-outcome counts, and immutable reward inputs, making each position's exact payout entitlement deterministic without enumerating every recipient. The amount never changes afterward and earns no interest, yield, bonus, or other time-based adjustment while unclaimed. Finalization does not attempt to transfer every payout in one transaction or claim that aggregate weight reveals the sum of every independently floored payout. It initializes an exact count of positive unclaimed positions; each successful claim decrements that count. A positive payout becomes `Claimable` immediately on authoritative PER finalization, potentially before the deterministic Solana `Resolved` projection lands; there is no cooldown or separate claim-opening transaction. A fully slashed position becomes terminal `Slashed` and requires no claim. Each eligible recipient must submit a wallet-authorized claim through the pinned onchain eSPL interface that transfers their finalized payout from the round's logical Vote Settlement Vault balance into the wallet's canonical reusable Private Voting Balance while atomically updating Opal's private claimed marker. Once the claim confirms, the credited USDC is immediately unlocked and may fund another Vote or be withdrawn; the claim creates no new lock or cooldown. The claim cannot redirect funds to another private balance or a public token account. Withdrawal from the canonical balance to a public Solana wallet remains a separate action. Settlement paths that end before voting keep their fixed, bounded automatic payouts.

**Why.** A Vote Round has no fixed maximum number of voters, while a Solana transaction has finite account, size, instruction, and compute limits. A single automatic transaction therefore cannot pay an unbounded recipient set. Required claims divide settlement into bounded, independently retryable transactions without an Opal-operated payout worker or an operator-liveness dependency. Every voter already creates and funds its Private Voting Balance before casting. A bonded participant that did not vote may create and delegate an empty canonical balance only when it has a payout to claim; it pays that destination's SOL setup costs but need not make a positive USDC deposit merely to receive the payout. Its missing destination never blocks finalization, and the entitlement remains reserved without expiry. Requiring the payout owner to sign also makes the delivery action explicit while preserving Opal's deterministic onchain calculation.

**Consequences.** A resolved Assertion can have unclaimed payouts, and a PER-authoritative claim may occur before the Solana `Resolved` projection. `Resolved` means the public truth outcome and payout formula are final, not that every participant has received USDC or that bounded publication of every public Vote position is complete. The public Vote Round carries the fixed three bonded-position payout projections, while each voter position eventually has its own permanent public account with its exact payout amount and `Claimable`, `Claimed`, or `Slashed` status; neither exposes a wallet's total Private Voting Balance. See [ADR-0017](0017-per-voter-public-position-accounts.md). A claim must validate the Vote Round, payout owner, positive entitlement, mint, configured Voting PER, and an already-existing canonically derived destination Private Voting Balance; it never creates the destination. Before claiming, the claimant creates and funds a fixed-size `ClaimReceipt` PDA on Solana and delegates it. Inside the PER, the USDC transfer, positive-unclaimed count, authoritative private claimed marker, and receipt write update atomically; the marker prevents a second payment. Commit-and-undelegate later restores the receipt for public synchronization—an event, ER-only Ephemeral Account, or hosted Private Payments request cannot replace it. Public bonded and Vote statuses are projections rather than double-claim authority. A matching restored claim receipt idempotently advances the appropriate bonded projection or existing `VotePositionAccount`. Position publication may create `Claimed` only when it also validates that restored receipt; if an unpublished claim has not yet been restored, publication may conservatively create stale `Claimable`, and later synchronization advances it without ever moving status backward. Arbitrary destinations and zero-value claims are invalid. Unclaimed USDC remains reserved in the logical Vote Settlement Vault balance with no forfeiture deadline, so the configured PER remains a post-finalization availability dependency. Devnet has no base-layer claim fallback, admin migration, or forfeiture path. The original wallet remains the only claim authority: Opal has no admin recovery, reassignment, or destination override if its signing key is lost. Any recovery must come from the wallet's own external recovery mechanism. Bond and Vote payouts remain separate claim records when one wallet owns both positions, but an integrating client may bundle both claim instructions into one transaction and wallet signature. Opal charges no additional claim fee and provides no protocol-funded sponsorship. The claimant pays destination setup and network execution costs unless an integrating application chooses to sponsor them; optional sponsorship does not remove the claimant's signature requirement or change claim ownership or amount.
